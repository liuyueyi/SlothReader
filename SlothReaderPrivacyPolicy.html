Optional Google Drive synchronization

Google Drive synchronization is optional. If you connect a Google account and enable synchronization, Sloth Reader uses the account email and available display name to show the connection and separate account-specific sync state. It requests the drive.file permission to access files created by, or explicitly made available to, the app. Reading data and files from the local synchronization directory are transferred directly to that account's Google Drive, using the SlothReader folder. This can include shelf metadata, favorites, tags, groups, reading positions, notes, bookmarks, actual reading duration, profile settings and book files stored in that directory. Book files elsewhere on the device are not automatically uploaded. You may explicitly select original books from your bookshelf for upload to SlothReader/BookLibrary, or download cloud originals into the local DownloadedBooks directory and bookshelf. These transfers use a separate queue; same-name originals are retained as separate copies. Private-book uploads use the existing private-reading authentication.

The developer does not operate a server for this reading-data synchronization. Disconnecting stops sync on this device and retains existing local and Drive data. You can revoke access through your Google account's third-party connections and remove cloud files in Google Drive. Access is used for synchronization requested by you, not for advertising. The app does not ask for your Google password or store an OAuth client secret in the app.

可选的 Google Drive 同步

Google Drive 同步为可选能力。连接 Google 账号并启用同步后，应用使用账号邮箱及可用显示名称展示连接，并区分账号同步状态。应用申请 drive.file 权限，访问由本应用创建或经用户明确交给应用的文件。本地同步目录中的书架信息、收藏、标签、分组、阅读位置、笔记、书签、真实阅读时长、用户配置及目录内书籍文件，会直接传输至该用户自己的 Google Drive，使用 SlothReader 文件夹；其他目录中的书籍原文件不会自动上传。你还可以主动从书架选择原文上传至 SlothReader/BookLibrary，或将云端原文下载到本地 DownloadedBooks 目录及书架；这些传输独立排队，同名文件另存副本，私密书籍上传沿用现有验证。

开发者不为此阅读数据同步运营服务器。断开连接会停止此设备同步，并保留已有本地及 Drive 数据。你可以在 Google 账号的第三方连接管理中撤销授权，并在 Drive 中删除云端文件。访问仅用于你要求的同步，不用于广告；应用不索取 Google 密码，也不在应用中保存 OAuth 客户端密钥。
