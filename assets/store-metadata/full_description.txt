Sloth Reader is a quiet place for the books, documents, and comics you already have. Keep your reading local, make your library your own, and come back to the page where you stopped.

READ YOUR WAY
• Browse books on your device and scan the folders you choose.
• Read PDF, EPUB, EPUB3, MOBI, DjVu, FB2, TXT, RTF, AZW, AZW3, HTML, CBZ, CBR, DOC, and DOCX files.
• Choose fonts, text size, spacing, colors, themes, and the page-turning style supported by each reader.
• Save bookmarks, highlights, and notes as you read.
• Listen with text-to-speech and use the built-in reading controls.
• Browse OPDS catalogs and use the backup, cloud storage, and synchronization options available in your distribution.

Sloth Reader is made for your personal reading library, with no account or book subscription required.
